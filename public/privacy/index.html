Privacy notice

Rocket cue is a free personal app by Kian Tajbakhsh. It does not require an account or run advertising or developer analytics. For privacy questions, email kian@tajbakhsh.dev.

Information on your device

Your Watchlist, selected place or current-location origin, sort and tab choices, map state, and cached launch and pad data stay in the app's local storage. You can remove Watchlist items and clear a saved origin in the app. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. Android cloud backup and device-to-device app-data extraction are disabled in the current Android version. Rocket cue does not provide an account or cross-device Watchlist synchronization.

Location and maps

Rocket cue requests location only when you choose current location as a distance origin. iPhone uses When In Use permission; Android requests foreground approximate-location permission for a one-time origin. It does not track your location in the background. Distances are calculated on your device, and your saved origin coordinates and Watchlist are not sent to the launch-data feed. You may deny location permission and use a manually chosen place or point instead.

On iPhone, Apple MapKit supplies maps and place search under Apple's privacy practices. On Android, city search uses Android Geocoder; your entered place name may be sent to the device's geocoding provider. You may instead enter coordinates or choose a point on the map.

Android light maps request raster tiles from OpenStreetMap, and dark maps request vector tiles, labels and symbols from OpenFreeMap. These requests include ordinary connection information such as your IP address and the map areas or resources you view. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and OpenFreeMap privacy policy, including its service-provider and security-log disclosures. Bundling the dark style does not make map tiles and labels fully available offline. Rocket cue does not add a user identifier or advertising SDK to these requests.

Launch data and hosting

The public app requests a read-only launch and pad cache over HTTPS from this GitHub Pages site. A scheduled updater obtains the source data from The Space Devs Launch Library 2. The updater sends its own network requests to that source; individual app users do not send their Watchlist or location to it through the feed. The app displays the last source-check time and may show older saved data when a refresh is delayed or fails.

GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security when it serves this website or the app's feed. GitHub handles that information under its privacy statement. Rocket cue's page code does not add analytics scripts, ads or its own cookies.

Images and external links

Some images are bundled in the app and make no separate network request. Rocket cue may request a source-supplied thumbnail over HTTPS from The Space Devs' known media host; that host receives the device's ordinary network request. Launch photos without acceptable reuse information are omitted. If you choose a webcast, it opens in another app or browser, whose own privacy practices apply. The app does not autoplay streams.

Calendar

On iPhone, Add to Calendar opens Apple's event editor with launch details. On Android, it opens an installed Calendar editor with the launch title, precise future time, region, source notes and an available stream link. Rocket cue for Android does not request calendar read or write permission. You decide whether to save the event, which Calendar account to use and which reminder to select in the editor. Your calendar provider controls any event synchronization or reminders. Rocket cue does not read your existing events, automatically update saved events after schedule changes, or run its own notification service.

Your choices and changes

You can use the schedule without location permission, clear the saved origin, remove Watchlist items, avoid external links or remove the app. For questions or corrections, email kian@tajbakhsh.dev. Third-party providers control their own processing and retention; their linked notices describe those practices. This notice will be updated if the app's data flows change. Android details were added on October 15, 2026; the prior version addressed iPhone.
