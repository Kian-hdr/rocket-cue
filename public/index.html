A personal app project

Follow the next liftoff.

Rocket cue brings upcoming rocket launches, launch-pad maps and a personal Watchlist into one clear place. It is free and has no account, ads or in-app purchases.

Available for iPhone. View Rocket cue on the App Store. The native Android app has been built and tested on a Galaxy S25 Ultra. Its public Google Play release is in preparation; the verified store link will appear here when available.

Browse launches

Search missions and filter by provider, location or target date.

Save a Watchlist

Keep interesting launches on your device without creating an account.

Explore pads

See launch facilities on native iPhone or Android maps. Pins are not public viewing entrances.

Open a webcast

Follow a source-listed stream when one is available, or add a launch to Calendar.

About the data

Launch schedules can change. Rocket cue shows when its shared copy of The Space Devs Launch Library 2 was last checked and keeps saved data available if an update fails. A scheduled refresh is best effort, so always verify time-sensitive launch details with the provider.
