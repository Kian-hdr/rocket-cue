Help and feedback

Rocket cue support

For app help, launch-data corrections or feedback, email kian@tajbakhsh.dev. Rocket cue is a free personal project by Kian Tajbakhsh.

Downloads

Download Rocket cue for iPhone and iPad on the App Store, or download the Android APK (1.0.0, build 8). The Android APK is a direct-install preview, not a Google Play release.

Install or update on Android

Android 8.0 or later is required. Download the APK from the link above and open it on your phone. If Android requests installation permission, allow it for the browser or file manager you chose. Follow the system installer prompts.

If Rocket cue is already installed, install this APK as an update. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling removes local app data. Build 8 uses the same signing certificate as build 7 and was tested as an update on a Galaxy S25 Ultra. If Android reports a signing mismatch, keep the installed app and contact support.

For SHA-256 checksums and tested behavior, see the release notes and download guide. Other Android devices and broader accessibility behavior are still being tested.

When reporting an issue

Please include the Rocket cue version from About, your phone model and iOS or Android version, what you expected, and what happened. For an old schedule, include the source-check time shown in About. Screenshots can help, but please remove private location or other sensitive details before sending them.

Launch information

Times, status, pads and webcast links may change. Rocket cue uses a scheduled shared copy of The Space Devs Launch Library 2 and can show saved data when a refresh fails. A map pin marks a launch facility, not a public viewing entrance or route. Confirm time-sensitive details with the launch provider or official webcast.

Accounts and payments

Rocket cue has no account, subscription, in-app purchase or advertising. The privacy notice explains local storage, location and network services.
